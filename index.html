Marvin

Inspired by the paranoid android from the Hitchhikers Guide to the Galaxy, Marvin is a personal assistant that automates what should be automated. Not what can be automated. This means that Marvin is a voice activated centralized control for your computer.

Marvin integrates into spotify, obsidian vault, can modify files, search the web, and help with time management. Marvin supports Grok, Claude, and ChatGPT api keys, meaning you don't sacrafice any quality for more features.

Lithium

A personal tool I think might be able to benefit more people. Lithium helps with time management by putting limits on websites, that you can adjust. Settings for custom presets help make it easier

To install, dowload the folder or clone it. Then go to chrome extensions, turn on developer mode, and simply load in the folder from load unpacked Lithium resides at the top bar in your mac.

Group Four

Group 4 uses Grok to group tabs based on context, not the type of tab. Everyone has a huge ammount of not sorted tabs on their browser. Group 4 groups them to optimise tabs.

To install, simply download or clone the folder from my git. Then go to chrome extensions, turn on developer mode, and load in the folder from load unpacked. Then pin the browser extension, click on it, and input your Grok API key. One click and it sorts all your tabs
